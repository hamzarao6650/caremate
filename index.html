How it works

It notices. It remembers. It talks.

One CareMate brain brings together a camera that reads how a person is, a memory of who they are and a voice to talk with them.

👁

Reads how they are

From an ordinary camera it tracks activity, posture against the person's own baseline, facial expression and who is in the room.

Pulse from the camera

Tiny changes in skin colour become a heart rate, with no wearable. Each reading is compared with that person's own 7-day range.

Privacy

Most of what it sees stays in the room.

Video is analysed on the device. Only small pieces of information go to the server, and visitors CareMate does not recognise leave no trace.

Processed on the device

  • Body pose and face mesh
  • Facial expression
  • Face recognition
  • Heart rate and its history
  • Activity and posture

Sent to the server

  • A face signature of 512 numbers, never a photo
  • A few small frames a minute, for a one-line scene description
  • Chat messages and each person's memory notes

FAQ

Common questions

Is CareMate a medical device?

No. CareMate's readings, including heart rate, are wellness signals. They are not medical measurements and have not yet been clinically validated. CareMate does not diagnose or treat any condition. In an emergency, call 911.

Do we need to buy special hardware?

No. The prototype runs in a web browser on an ordinary laptop webcam. CareMate is software designed to run on the cameras, tablets and speakers a care provider already has, with room cameras and sensors planned next.

Does CareMate record or stream video?

Video is analysed on the device. CareMate sends only a 512-number face signature (never a photo), a few small frames a minute for a one-line scene description, and chat messages and memory notes. Carers receive short updates and a daily summary, not a video feed.

Does CareMate detect falls today?

Today the prototype reads activity, including when a person is lying down, as well as posture, emotion and pulse, and it talks with the person. Fall detection, urgent alerts and the carer app are on the near-term roadmap.

What about bathrooms and private spaces?

Cameras do not belong everywhere. For places like bathrooms, the plan is to use non-camera sensors instead.

How does CareMate remember each person?

CareMate keeps a separate memory for every person it knows, built from their conversations. Face recognition picks whose memory to use, and unrecognised visitors get no profile. Memories are plain notes that staff can read, search, correct and export.

Is CareMate HIPAA compliant?

Not yet. CareMate is a prototype. A security review is part of the pilot-ready stage, and a HIPAA-ready stack is planned before wider rollout.

How can our organisation try it?

We are looking for care homes and home-care agencies to run a 90-day pilot on the devices they already have, measuring response times, incidents and staff hours against their own baseline. Use the CareMate assistant on this page to request a pilot.
